— And it doesn’t stop there

And many small automationsthat give you your day back.

  • No lead left behind

    An alert when a lead has gone quiet for too many days, or has sat too long in the same stage.

  • Leads from everywhere, in one place

    Instagram forms, websites and partners flow in on their own. WhatsApp messages land on the right lead.

  • Never forget to call again

    A reminder when a client goes too long without contact, plus birthdays, Christmas and Easter.

  • AI tells you the next step

    It qualifies every lead, suggests the next action and writes the closing email for everyone involved.

  • Clients choose without calling you

    A private portal where each client sees the recommended properties and says what they like.

  • Publish once, appear everywhere

    Properties go to the property portals on their own, through an automatic feed.

  • Rules that work for you

    A deal changes stage and the CRM creates the task, alerts the right person or sends the email or WhatsApp.

  • Owners always looked after

    An alert when an owner with an active listing goes 10 days without hearing from you.

  • Your day starts organised

    At 8 am the day’s tasks arrive. Your calendar and contacts stay in sync with your phone.
